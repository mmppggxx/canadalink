About us

CanadaLink is a Toronto-based provider of secure, high-performance private network solutions, specializing in 5G and AI-enabled connectivity for industrial and rural sectors. We support Canadian businesses in mining, agriculture, logistics, and smart infrastructure with mission-critical, locally managed networks that ensure data sovereignty and operational independence.

Our technology is built on the principles of the Open Radio Access Network (O-RAN), enabling a modular and vendor-neutral architecture that contrasts sharply with traditional, closed systems from legacy providers. This open approach promotes interoperability, cost efficiency, and local innovation.

CanadaLink also integrates AI-RAN capabilities—leveraging artificial intelligence to optimize radio resource management, automate network functions, and adapt to real-time conditions—making our private 5G networks smarter, more resilient, and easier to manage.

As we expand into international markets such as Southeast Asia, we bring a future-ready solution that aligns with local spectrum frameworks and industrial demands. Whether deployed as a DIY kit or through a fully managed service, CanadaLink delivers the agility and intelligence modern enterprises need.

We combine Canadian leadership, open standards, and AI innovation to power the next generation of private wireless networks—securely and globally.

CanadaLink is a member of SimpleRAN community and associated with Rapid.Space.
